Dental care tips

Brush twice a day

Two minutes, soft bristles, fluoride toothpaste. Replace the brush every three months.

Clean between teeth

Floss or use interdental brushes once a day; brushing alone misses a third of each tooth.

Cut down on sugar

How often matters more than how much. Keep sweets to mealtimes, not snacks through the day.

After a root canal

Avoid chewing on that side until the crown is placed. A temporary filling can break.

After an extraction

Bite on gauze for 30 minutes. No spitting, straws or smoking for 24 hours. Cold packs help swelling.

See us every six months

Scaling and a check-up catch problems while they are small and cheaper to fix.
